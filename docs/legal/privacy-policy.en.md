# Privacy Policy — WarrantyVault

**Last updated:** May 23, 2026

## Summary

WarrantyVault is a warranty management app that runs **100% on your device**. We do not collect, store or transmit your data to any server.

## 1. Data the app stores

The following data is stored **only on your device** (local database and the app's private storage):

- Product information: name, category, store, price, purchase date, warranty end date and notes.
- Photos of receipts or invoices (only if you choose to add them).
- App preferences: theme, language and notification status.

This data **never leaves your device**. We do not have servers nor any access to your information.

## 2. Permissions the app requests

- **Notifications (Android 13+):** to send you reminders when a warranty is about to expire.
- **Billing (Google Play Billing):** to process Premium subscriptions through Google Play.

The app **does not request the Internet permission**, so it is technically unable to send your data outside the device.

## 3. Premium subscriptions

Premium subscription purchases are processed through **Google Play Billing**. WarrantyVault does not receive or store payment information (card numbers, banking details, etc.). Payment processing and billing data are subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

WarrantyVault only receives from Google Play the confirmation that your subscription is active.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

**We do not share your data with anyone.** The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising services. The only external communication is with Google Play, exclusively to validate subscriptions.

## 5. Your rights

- **Access your data:** everything is inside the app, on your device.
- **Delete your data:** uninstalling the app removes absolutely all data (database, photos and preferences) from your device. No copy exists anywhere else.
- **Android automatic backup:** if you have Google Backup enabled on your account, Android may include this app's data in its encrypted backup. You can exclude it in *Settings → System → Backup*.

## 6. Children

WarrantyVault does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is intended for a general adult audience.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version at this same URL with the updated date at the top.
